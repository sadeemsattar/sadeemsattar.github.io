## The problem

Product-led growth teams drown in events but starve for answers. Journey maps,
cohort cuts, and churn signals exist in the data, but assembling them by hand is
slow enough that retention work is always a post-mortem.

## The approach

An AI system that automates the analyst workflow end-to-end:

- **Journey mapping & cohort analysis** over product-analytics events
- **Tree-based segmentation** to pinpoint the drivers of user drop-off
- **Churn-prediction pipeline** over behavioral + billing events to flag at-risk
  users before they leave
- **BERTopic** modeling over qualitative feedback from Intercom, Typeform, and
  Zendesk to surface recurring friction points

## Outcome

Raw product and billing events become a ranked list of at-risk users, so
retention becomes proactive instead of a post-mortem.
